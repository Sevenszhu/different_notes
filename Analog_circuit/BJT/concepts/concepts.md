# Concepts in BJT

1. When determining the type of a BJT, simply focus on the arrow at its emitter terminal. Since the base-emitter junction behaves like a diode, you can use the direction of the emiiter current ($I_E$) to determine whether the emmiter terminal is an N-type or P-type.  Thus, you can identify whether the BJT is a PNP or NPN. For learners, this approach is more effective than simply memorizing the arrow direction because it also helps them understand the equivalent diode model used in DC analysis.

2. $\beta = \frac{i_C}{i_B}$ is the **common-emitter current gain** of the BJT.

3. $\alpha = \frac{i_C}{i_E}$ is the **common-base current gain** of the BJT.
